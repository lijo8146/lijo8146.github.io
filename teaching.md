<section><h2>Learning through environmental questions</h2><p>I teach Earth data science at CIRES Earth Lab at CU Boulder. My teaching connects scientific programming with environmental questions, from working with geospatial data to communicating the results of a reproducible analysis.</p><p>I develop graduate, undergraduate, and professional curricula, open tutorials, and computational learning resources. My work includes the Graduate Earth Data Science Certificate, ESIIL Stars, short courses, workshops, and hackathons.</p></section>
