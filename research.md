<section><h2>Questions grounded in place</h2><p>My research combines remote sensing, geostatistics, machine learning, and hydrologic modeling to investigate environmental systems. I build reproducible Python workflows that connect observations, spatial analysis, and interpretation.</p>
<article class="entry"><p class="meta">2026–present · Principal Investigator · CIRES Innovative Research Program</p><h3>Shadow Ecology: Model Residuals as Signal</h3><p>Can spatial patterns in model residuals reveal missing environmental processes, observation gaps, or limitations in land-cover labels? This study focuses on the Walker River region of Nevada. Planned analyses combine satellite observations and land-cover models with spatial residual diagnostics and tests of environmental associations.</p><a href="https://github.com/lijo8146/shadow-ecology">Explore the research repository →</a></article>
<article class="entry"><p class="meta">2025–present · Oglala Lakota College Geospatial Data Hub</p><h3>Community Earth Data Infrastructure</h3><p>Through Daear Consulting, I develop geospatial pipelines under an Oglala Lakota College USDA NIFA award. The work brings Earth observation, hydrologic, ecological, and climate datasets into reusable Python workflows and educational resources.</p><p>I created the sovereignty-embedded Earth Data Science (SEEDS) framework for Tribal communities. Data cube integration and public dashboards are planned as a subsequent development stage.</p><a href="https://github.com/daearconsulting">Explore Daear Consulting’s work →</a></article>
<article class="entry"><p class="meta">2025–present · Founder and program lead</p><h3>Tribal Fire Science Initiative</h3><p>I develop open wildfire data infrastructure using satellite observations, geospatial analytics, and reproducible Python workflows. The initiative brings together partnerships, educational materials, workshops, and hackathons to support wildfire planning and climate adaptation with Tribal Nations.</p></article>
<article class="entry"><p class="meta">2022–2024 · CIRES Earth Lab · Project and technical lead</p><h3>Predictive Fuel Management and Wildfire Risk</h3><p>In collaboration with Southern California Edison and interdisciplinary CIRES researchers, I led technical development of workflows integrating Sentinel-2 imagery, vegetation indices, spectral analysis, and machine learning to investigate fuel conditions and elevated wildfire risk in Southern California.</p></article>
<article class="entry"><p class="meta">2013–2021 · Doctoral research · Pine Ridge Reservation</p><h3>Groundwater Vulnerability Assessment</h3><p>My doctoral research integrated hydrogeologic observations from more than 1,500 wells with environmental and geospatial datasets to assess aquifer vulnerability to contamination. I applied kriging, fuzzy logic, and machine learning to characterize spatial variation and support groundwater assessment.</p></article></section>
<section><h2>Research foundations</h2><p>I earned my PhD in Geology and Geological Engineering at South Dakota School of Mines and Technology in 2021. My dissertation examined aquifer vulnerability to contamination on the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation in southwestern South Dakota. From 2018 to 2020, I was a visiting fellow in NCAR’s Climate and Global Dynamics Laboratory, working with machine learning and high-performance computing in Earth system science.</p><p><a href="/publications/">Publications and selected presentations →</a></p></section>
